# Halloween hold qualification

The three expired holds are now integrated locally: Hat-and-Capelet Pikachu, Top-Hat Zubat, and Bow Sinistea. Four explicit selectable identities are added; Sinistea Phony and Antique have different canonical IDs. A fifth rendering mapping preserves historical `Sinistea (Bow)` as **form unspecified**, with no alias or saved-data rewrite into either form. The source explicitly assigns the same front artwork to both forms; the hidden stamp is not visible.

Ten original PNGs serve runtime: four gender/colour variants each for Pikachu and Zubat, plus normal/Shiny Sinistea. All originals were decoded and visually inspected, with published page-to-model gender metadata and SHA256 provenance. Pikachu male straight versus female heart tail and Zubat fang differences were checked. Dittobase pages explicitly publish every exact asset URL; no filename guesses, generated/recoloured pixels, generic species substitution or remote runtime fetch is used.

Gender is an optional trade preference. Unspecified remains an empty saved field and uses the default exact costume image; an explicit female preference selects female artwork. Artwork never creates a gender requirement. Sinistea remains genderless. The existing legacy-invalid-qualifier handling is unchanged.

Official release window is October27 10a.m. **local** through November1 10a.m. local. At source review, the scheduled window had begun in NZ/Kiribati and was still future in New York. Official announcement and current Serebii event/Shiny listings establish regional availability of all three costumes and their Shinies. This is not a claim of worldwide UTC-midnight activation or first-hand game encounter capture. Dittobase still showed an **Unreleased** badge: its artwork/model metadata is used, but that cached badge is not the release oracle. This conflict is retained in the evidence rather than hidden.

Sources and retrieval hashes are in `data/halloween2026-art-review.json`. Original Serebii references remain as independent visual evidence. The current official review supplement from `38187c62` is reused, and a new explicit corroborated review closes the three obsolete announced/unconfirmed holds. Historical review evidence and original expiry remain unchanged. No freshness checker condition or permission is weakened.

Existing396catalogue records and their normal artwork remain byte-for-byte semantically unchanged; the seven unrelated unsupported identities remain held.
