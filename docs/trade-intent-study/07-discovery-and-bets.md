# Useful people, not a bigger directory

## North-star opportunity

Help a trainer identify a person worth contacting and arrive with an accurate,
two-sided plan. 'Mutual matching' alone is not differentiated: see S4-S6 in the
[market notebook](01-research.md). The proposed advantage is the combination of
truthful variants, permissioned reach, current availability and frictionless handoff.
10x is an ambition to test, not a measured performance or market claim.

## Deterministic qualification before ranking

1. Authorization/publication gate: only current consented projections. Private,
   revoked, blocked, stale-version and ineligible projections do not enter counts.
   Don't fan out private account reads to assemble a discovery index.
2. Context gate: user-selected community or coarse area; location optional. Remote
   candidates only under a separately verified game-eligibility context, never
   inferred from a friend code. No GPS collection, exact home marker, public last-seen.
3. Variant gate: species/form, shiny status and requested gender/Max/BG constraints.
   Names and artwork are display only in the production design; stable IDs are keys.
4. Offer gate: only explicit current offers. Registered/owned/wanted/not wanted
   are not offers. Unknown fields produce 'confirm details', not exact counts.
5. Freshness tier: recently confirmed declarations above stale ones. Confirmation
   means reviewing availability, not merely opening the app. Stale lists remain
   inspectable under an explicit filter; never auto-delete someone's intent.
6. Reciprocal tier: both directions above one-way interest. Show both counts and
   actual examples, never their sum as a meaningful 'score'.
7. Within tier: match the viewer's top wants, then balanced breadth using the smaller
   of the two directional counts; cap repeated variants so a huge list cannot swamp
   a precise collector. Saved relationship and recent confirmation break ties;
   stable trainer ID is final tie-break. No pay-to-rank, rarity pricing or AI.

Prototype intentionally implements a smaller fixture matcher: exact attributes,
any-BG asymmetry, separate uncertain results, freshness and smaller-direction
ranking. It is not a production discovery/privacy index or an exhaustive scheduler.

## Why this person, why now?

Show 'You receive: these three; You give: these two', chosen community and declared
confirmation age. Explain missing evidence: exact BG not supplied, offer old, no
reciprocal offer, contact method absent. Distinguish zero from unavailable and from
hidden. Do not frame one-sided interest as failure; it can still be useful with a
friend, but it is a different result tier.

Two independent sets are not a negotiated bundle. Session selection can choose
from each, then the trainer asks availability. A matching costume with wrong gender
must not masquerade as an exact match. Any-BG want can accept an exact BG offer;
an unknown/any-BG offer cannot prove an exact BG want. Historic free-text qualifiers
require confirmation until losslessly typed, never silent normalization.

## Freshness without a maintenance chore

Review changed offers when returning, share with a 'confirmed' date, and after a
session offer a deliberate 'Review my offers' action. No automatic deletion after
checkbox completion. Suggest review only for relevant stale offers; avoid nagging
all 300 entries. Preview renewal differences before publication. Opt-out hides a
trainer from discovery without deleting their list or private saved relationships.
Thresholds are configurable product hypotheses (prototype uses fixture labels),
not a reason to introduce an automatic messaging campaign now.

## Small-network behavior

Zero mutuals: keep editing, sharing and contextual search useful. Offer anonymous
recipient self-selection without account creation or storing their answers remotely.
After value is visible, account save protects a draft and lets it work across devices.
No forced signup to reveal matches already computed from public data. Saved people
is one private relationship layer, not Favorites plus Recents plus subscriptions.
Publishing a list, joining discovery and exposing a contact method require separate
clear consent; none implies the others. Public image disclosure is irreversible.

## Measurement plan

Primary: participant identifies a contact-worthy reciprocal opportunity and prepares
correct searches without manual list reconstruction. Observe task time and error
types; don't collect raw searches, Pokémon lists, exact location or conversations
in analytics. Track anonymous aggregate funnel counts only after a privacy review.
Separately measure zero-result rate, ambiguous-match rejection and reconfirmation
burden. Compare against the participant's current screenshot/DM workflow, not an
invented competitor timing. Sample collectors and casual local traders separately.

## Ranked product bets

Directional value x frequency x differentiation / complexity, not numeric theatre.

| Rank | Bet | Value / frequency / distinction / cost | Evidence & gating |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contextual, honest search for selected intent and both match sides | High / repeated / meaningful / low-medium | Existing serializer strength; S7 manual query work. Test exact limitations comprehension |
| 2 | Fresh, qualified reciprocal people in chosen community | High / repeated / combination advantage / high | S4-S6 establish parity; consent/index and liquidity gates required |
| 3 | One shared editor and scope-first share | High / every creation / parity plus lower burden / medium | Current shipped foundation, S1-S4 low-friction makers |
| 4 | Anonymous recipient self-check | High / every received link / strong low-network value / medium | Avoid both-users-installed requirement; no hidden account data |
| 5 | Private trade preparation with two searches | High at meetups / episodic / combination advantage / medium | S6 is competing evidence; no negotiations ledger |
| 6 | Entry-level freshness review after real sessions | Medium-high / repeated / useful / medium | No background notifications until usage justifies them |
| 7 | Archive then retire live legacy paths | Indirect high / ongoing / internal simplification / high | Data inventory, verified restore and rollback gates first |

Table stakes: accurate catalog, exact imagery when permitted, fast mobile add,
accessible density, local-language query output, public share and dependable save.
Existing differentiators worth protecting: semantic data preservation, explicit
offers, reviewed sprites, bounded search compiler, privacy-aware recipient path.
False complexity: multi-page 'tools', duplicate Board records, global inventory
scores, many saved concepts, collection-type navigation, permanent dual-write paths.
Missing workflows: useful strangers without name lookup; exact-vs-possible evidence;
anonymous self-check; agreed-context handoff from reciprocal view into game search.
