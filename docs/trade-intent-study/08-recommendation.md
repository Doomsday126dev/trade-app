# Recommended product: My List + People

## One sentence

PoGo Trades turns explicit wants and offers into useful conversations and an
accurate plan for the next Pokemon GO trade.

Primary user: a trainer who trades with a community or recurring friends, spanning
casual species/XL needs and exact collectible wants. Do not optimize exclusively
for a global rare-costume marketplace or a full-Dex completion database.

First session: create a private want or offer, see it immediately, add another,
and produce a useful share or search. Sign-in becomes valuable for durable account
save and publication; legacy PIN and same-UID behavior remain unchanged until a
separately authorized identity phase. This study does not activate any provider.

## Visible model

- My List: explicit declarations, each wanting/offering a reviewed variant plus
  structured constraints. No physical specimen inventory or quantities.
- People: consented trading intent, saved privately by viewer, context and confirmation
  freshness. Not a social feed or a complete public identity directory.
- Trade preparation: a temporary private selection, not ownership transfer, partner
  acceptance or another editable collection.

Two primary destinations. Account menu for settings, advanced import/export,
existing events and owner-only maintenance. Detail sheets for add/edit/share/search.
Public recipient route stands alone. Draft session opens contextually from a match.

## Screens and behavior

My List shows Looking For and For Trade together. Each is a compact sprite grid,
with temporary All/Wants/Offers and collectible filters. Compact rows are the
accessible/detailed alternative and show both declarations together. Species names
are available via labels/detail, not forced underneath every exported sprite.
Exact BG text is never hidden when no approved art is available. Top want is subtle;
full historic tiers stay in advanced details until the owner explicitly changes them.

People gives deterministic, explained reciprocal candidates from permitted scope.
No partner? Share still works. No current offers? Do not manufacture them. Viewing
a person opens independent You give / You receive sets and a Prepare action.
Account-backed anonymous comparisons would reuse authorized published data, not
leak private sync state. The prototype uses synthetic fixtures only.

Share is scope then output: the same entries become a public preview, image or
text. Publication freshness and local draft freshness stay distinct. Board curation
is a selection/preset over source IDs. It is not a second editable Pokemon model.

Search belongs wherever the trainer is about to switch to Pokemon GO. Preserve
existing serializer/localization; do not import historic restrictive filters into
all contexts. Show coarse prefilters and what remains a manual check. Special
collectibles, Max and future dimensions stay typed attributes behind disclosure.

Events provide meeting context and candidate scope. They do not own trade intent,
require calendar enrollment or create separate event copies of lists. Private notes
and saved people remain private. Profile/avatar/code do not precede the first add.

## Visual system

Quiet neutral canvas, green offers/actions, berry wants, Pokemon imagery as the
main color. Flat sections, one-pixel dividers, compact typography and stable touch
geometry. No giant dashboard hero, decorative gradient, nested cards or themed BG
facsimile. Desktop gains wider grids; it does not enlarge each entry into a card.
Mobile has a bottom Add action and stable navigation. Badge placement is based on
visible artwork bounds, with reserved space and separate gender alignment.

## What disappears

Board and Compare as destinations; inventory/mirror counters; permanent categories
for Max/costume/BG; separate Recents; public profile setup as onboarding; standalone
search tools. Existing useful data survives in advanced detail or archive, not
forced into primary navigation. Admin remains owner-only. No new internal chat,
ratings, AI, market prices, provider activation or source migration in this study.

## Open hypotheses, not blockers to this study

Will 5-column mobile sprites remain recognizable for rare costumes? Does the row
alternative solve accessibility without fragmenting the model? Do users interpret
two-sided candidates as equal-value swaps despite the wording? What minimum offer
confirmation is tolerable? How much useful liquidity exists in each community?
Prototype observation must answer these before calling the product superior.
