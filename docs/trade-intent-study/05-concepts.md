# Three information architectures, before convergence

All three use explicit want/offer intent. They differ in default object, navigation,
creation trigger and sharing unit, not just colors. Prototype launch links expose
each structure with the same synthetic catalog for comparison.

## A. Collection first: My List / People

Default: own two-sided collection. Main action: Add Pokemon. One picker, independent
want/offer choice; both sections visible without an editing-mode switch. Filters
provide temporary focus. Share lives in list header and selection toolbar. People
shows eligible mutual opportunities, then clearly separate one-way/uncertain ones.
Opening a person reveals reciprocal candidates; Prepare creates a private draft.
Account avatar contains settings/admin, advanced import and existing events.
No Board, Compare, separate Recents, top-level Events, or collectible category pages.

Strength: useful even with no network. Weakness: opportunity discovery costs one
navigation action; mitigate with one compact relevant-person link, not a feed.

## B. Opportunity first: For You / Collection / Saved

Default: ranked reciprocal people. Main action: respond to a useful opportunity.
List building is inline 'Add what you want' and 'Add an offer' prompts when signals
are absent. LF/FT shown in paired opportunity rows; collection is a compact table.
Share starts with 'this person' or current opportunity, whole-list share secondary.
Account hosts settings/admin/events; no independent Board/Compare/calendar.

Strength: fastest returning-network discovery. Weakness: empty-network cold start;
can falsely imply coverage or offer availability. Higher publication/index/privacy
burden. Distinct test: no-data state must not invent matches or force location.

## C. Session first: Today / People / Collection

Default: next private trade plan with two-sided checklist. Main action: Prepare a
trade. Creation begins from partner or meetup, choosing Pokemon as part of plan.
People is partner chooser; list is reusable source material. Share sends the plan
or a compact message; whole collection share is secondary. Settings/admin under
account. Events become optional meeting labels; no stand-alone Board or Compare.

Strength: best at an actual meetup and repeated XL sessions. Weakness: asks new
collectors for a partner/time before they know either; risks turning drafts into
another permanent negotiation ledger. Proposal does not equal partner acceptance.

## Decision comparison (directional, no fabricated scores)

| Criterion | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| First useful list | Best: direct | Extra cold-start prompts | Poor without partner |
| Cognitive load | Two durable destinations | Feed + signals to maintain | Session vs collection distinction |
| Mobile speed | Strong everyday editing | Best repeat match discovery | Best at meetup |
| Density | Grid/rows + compact people | Match pairs repeat imagery | Focused checklist |
| Anonymous recipient | Natural full intent | Better with known viewer | Useful only for invited partner |
| Hundreds of entries | Strong filter/paging | Collection still required | Plan subset hides overall backlog |
| Implementation | Reuse current declarations | New consented discovery index | New draft state and lifecycle |
| Legacy reduction | Removes surface duplication | Can add feed caches | Can add plan duplicates |
| Differentiation | Low alone; strong last-mile | Crowded 'mutual match' claim | Useful but smaller audience |

## Alternatives on the hard decisions

| Decision | Explored alternatives | Convergence |
|---|---|---|
| LF/FT | Separate modes; per-row two checkboxes; two simultaneous sections | Two sections + shared editor; row matrix retained as dense alternate |
| Discovery | Global species directory; ranked mutual people; session partner picker | Consent-scoped mutual people, name search fallback, no global assumption |
| Match | Single total; paired fixed swaps; independent give/receive sets | Independent sets with optional draft selection; no fabricated fair exchange |
| Share | Export wizard; share sheet; per-session message | One scope-first sheet, image/text/link outputs, session message contextual |
| Large lists | Infinite tiles; compact table; paged hybrid | 60-entry pages + search/filter + optional rows; measured later before virtualization |
| Shiny/gender | Text pills; badges on sprite; reserved icon geometry | Minimal sparkle, aligned symbol box outside visible art; no F/M or mirror |

## Recommendation

Choose A, borrow B's qualified People ranking and C's private preparation drawer.
Do not promote a session tab until recurring use warrants it. Two durable things:
my trading intent and useful people. Sharing and preparation are verbs, not more
collections. This is a recommendation from evidence and prototype inspection,
not a claim of validated superiority. Prototype feedback gates implementation.
